Collision Risk & Proximity Heuristic Analysis
⚠️
Important Safety Classification Standard: All telemetry outputs produced by this system represent Estimated Collision Risks based on 2D camera geometry and bounding-box proximity. In strict accordance with emergency operations protocols, risk indicators do not constitute confirmed physical accidents until verified by a qualified watch officer.

Geometric Risk Matrix

Risk Tier Mathematical Metric Response Protocol
HIGH RISK IoU ≥ 0.15 OR Dist < 35px Immediate EOCC alert + Dispatch standby
MEDIUM RISK IoU ≥ 0.04 OR Dist < 75px Logged proximity hazard warning
LOW RISK Centroid Dist ≥ 75px Standard highway traffic monitoring

Mathematical Foundation

Intersection over Union (IoU):
IoU(A, B) = Area(A ∩ B) / Area(A ∪ B)
Quantifies overlapping 2D area between vehicles or obstacles.
Euclidean Centroid Distance:
Distance = √((cx₁ - cx₂)² + (cy₁ - cy₂)²)
Measures spatial clearance between vehicle centroids and pedestrian coordinates.

The Four Core Modules

1. Video Processing

Extracts high-resolution frames from surveillance cameras, IP RTSP streams, and recorded video files via OpenCV.

2. Vehicle & Object Detection

YOLOv8 network performs single-stage bounding-box classification for cars, buses, trucks, motorcycles, bicycles, and pedestrians.

3. Accident Analysis

Spatial heuristic engine evaluates IoU overlapping percentages and centroid proximity vectors to classify collision risks.

4. Alert & Response Dashboard

Command center interface providing real-time incident audit logs, video streaming, and simulated emergency response signals.
